## 2. My findings

### Finding 1 — The displayed quote direction does not match the conversion direction I selected

- Where: https://mgmt6110-betterrate.vercel.app/, result card after selecting VND in “I HAVE”, SGD in “I WANT”, choosing the 7-day average, and pressing “Compare today’s rate”.
- What I did, what I saw: I selected VND → SGD and compared today’s rate. The conclusion said “Better for converting VND to SGD”, but the rate rows were quoted in the reverse direction: “1 SGD = 20,295.77 VND”, “1 SGD = 20,366.36 VND”, and “−70.58 VND per SGD”. I had to mentally reverse the quote to connect it to the exchange direction I had selected.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World.
- Screen or system: Screen. The page already knows the selected FROM and TO currencies and already has the calculated rate, so it can present the result in the visitor’s selected direction without needing a different provider response.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by impact and market impact. This is the main decision result, so presenting the quote in the opposite direction can make a first-time visitor misread the answer or lose trust even when the underlying calculation is correct.
- The repair: The primary result should quote today’s rate, the benchmark and the difference in the same FROM → TO direction the visitor selected, or make the quote direction unmistakably explicit without requiring mental inversion.

### Finding 2 — The primary decision does not show when today’s rate was refreshed

- Where: https://mgmt6110-betterrate.vercel.app/, primary result card after a successful comparison.
- What I did, what I saw: I compared a currency pair and saw today’s rate, the selected benchmark and the percentage difference, but the primary result did not show when the current rate had last been refreshed. I could not tell from the answer itself how fresh “today’s rate” was.
- Which heuristic: 1, Visibility of System Status.
- Screen or system: Screen. The current-rate API response already includes a `lastRefreshed` value, so the page has the information needed to show freshness without changing the provider or calculation.
- Severity, and why: 2, driven by impact. The result remains usable, but exchange rates can change during the day and freshness affects how confidently a visitor can use the comparison.
- The repair: The decision area should show a concise freshness indicator such as “Updated [time/date]” using the timestamp already returned by the API.

### Finding 3 — The benchmark description can be read as more precise than the calculation actually is

- Where: https://mgmt6110-betterrate.vercel.app/, “Compare against” section before running a comparison.
- What I did, what I saw: The 7-day option says “Average of daily close rates over the last 7 calendar days” and the 30-day option uses the same wording for 30 days. In the implemented calculation, today is excluded and weekends/non-trading days are not filled with artificial observations, so a 7-day benchmark may use about five prior daily closes rather than seven observations.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World.
- Screen or system: Screen. The calculation already knows which historical observations are used; the problem is that the visible wording can give a different mental model of the benchmark.
- Severity, and why: 2, driven by persistence. The wording appears every time a visitor chooses a benchmark and can repeatedly create the assumption that seven or thirty daily observations are always being averaged.
- The repair: Keep the simple 7-day and 30-day labels, but describe them as averages of available prior daily closes within the selected window, with today excluded; the full definition can remain inside calculation details.

### Finding 4 — “Check another rate” duplicates controls that are already available on the same page

- Where: https://mgmt6110-betterrate.vercel.app/, below the decision card and the two collapsed evidence sections.
- What I did, what I saw: After I received a result, the currency selectors and benchmark controls were still visible above it, but there was also a separate “Check another rate” action below. I could already change the currencies or benchmark using the controls on the same page, so the extra action did not clearly add a new capability.
- Which heuristic: 8, Aesthetic and Minimalist Design.
- Screen or system: Screen. The issue is only the visible page structure and reset/scroll action; no new system data is needed.
- Severity, and why: 1, driven by impact. It does not prevent the task, but it adds another action to a product whose main value is a quick comparison.
- The repair: Remove the extra action if the visible selectors are sufficient, or make it a clearly useful shortcut back to the input without duplicating the comparison workflow.
